# Roadmap

This document tracks completed and planned milestones for SABRINA. Every Lean module builds with zero `sorry` and strictly standard Lean axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`), checked by `scripts/verify_axioms.py`.

## Completed milestones

- **A. Strict W3C §11.2 validator** (`StrictOWL2DL.lean`, `8e8a679`): `--strict-owl2dl` and `"strict_owl2dl": true` enforcing `owl:topDataProperty` placement, anonymous-individual forest and single-named-root restrictions, XSD 1.1 pattern syntax, and regular/acyclic role hierarchies.
- **B. Declarations, punning, FSS, and import catalogs** (`FunctionalSyntax.lean`, `StrictTyping.lean`, `ImportCatalog.lean`, `426516b`): `Declaration(...)` and annotation-axiom parsing, prefixed ontology/version IRIs, Unicode and `PN_LOCAL_ESC` tokens, W3C §5.8.1–§5.8.2 typing and punning checks, and OASIS `catalog-v001.xml` / `--import-dir` loading.
- **C.1. Unicode 17.0.0 XSD 1.1 regular expressions** (`UnicodeCategories.lean`, `8e8a679`): `\d`/`\D`, `\w`/`\W`, `\p{...}`/`\P{...}` categories and blocks, nested character-class subtraction, and full-Unicode boundary sampling.
- **C.2. Standards correspondence** (`StandardsCorrespondence.lean`, `XMLCorrespondence.lean`, `RegexGrammarCorrespondence.lean`, `65b241f`): independent declarative specifications and correspondence theorems for `xsd:hexBinary`, IEEE-754 ties-to-even rounding, `xsd:dateTime`, RFC 4647 language ranges, Exclusive XML Canonicalization, and the XSD 1.1 Appendix G regex grammar. Renamed primary CLI to `sabrina-query`.
- **D. RDF/XML, Turtle, reasoning services, and W3C suite** (`RDF/`, `ExtendedQueryDecision.lean`, `Classification.lean`, `benchmarks/w3c-owl2-conformance/`, `a0ca51b`): native RDF 1.1 graph, RDF/XML 1.1 and Turtle 1.1 parsers, W3C OWL 2 RDF-to-Structural mapping with round-trip proof, 4-way entailment across 29 axiom forms and anonymous-individual queries, certified TBox classification and ABox realization, and a 42-test conformance suite.
- **E.1. Hash-indexed $\bot$-locality extraction** (`FastLocality.lean`, `benchmarks/fast-locality/`, `e8bd449`): inverted signature index, worklist fixpoint, and linear certificate verifier proved axiom-set-identical to `Profile.extractBotModule` (`extractBotModuleFast_mem_iff`, `extractBotModuleFast_entails_iff`), wired into `sabrina-query` and cached in `sabrina-session`.
- **E.2. Release hygiene** (`v1.0.0`): package versions aligned to `1.0.0`, documentation updated, and `v1.0.0` tagged on GitHub.
- **E.3. Materialization, fast classification, and minimal justifications** (`Materialize.lean`, `RDF/Serialize.lean`, `FastClassification.lean`, `Justification.lean`, `v1.1.0`): kernel-verified inferred OWL export (`.ofn`/`.ttl`/`.owl`), certified hybrid TBox classification, and QuickXplain + Hitting-Set Tree minimal justifications.
- **E.4. Parallel $\mathcal{EL}^{++}$ engine, RDF metadata, and exact reasoning scaling** (`FastEL.lean`, `OWLMapping.lean`, `WiderDataReduction.lean`, `DataRangeExact.lean`, `DataCSP.lean`, `SROIQOps.lean`, `SROIQSearch.lean`, `af2d378`, `v1.1.1`, `ca72069`): parallel integer-interned single-model $\mathcal{EL}^{++}$ classification (350k classes), W3C Table 4/5 ontology-header annotations and two-phase import-closure declaration sharing, kernel-verified pruned Venn partitions and empty-interval filtering, indexed role closure and tableau initialization, and streamed materialization.
- **F. Kernel-Verified DL-Safe SWRL & Non-EL Performance (`v1.2.0`)** (`SWRL.lean`, `SWRLBuiltins.lean`, `SWRLReduction.lean`, `SROIQD/Model.lean`, `SROIQD/Search.lean`, `SROIQD/Termination/SROIQSearch.lean`, `Classification.lean`, `FastClassification.lean`, `Justification.lean`, `Materialize.lean`, `StrictTyping.lean`, `tests/SabrinaTests/SWRLTests.lean`, `1942cad`–`17fdf35`): orthogonal DL-Safe SWRL AST, Direct Semantics, and DL-safety validator; FSS, Turtle, and RDF/XML rule parsing/serialization (`owl2_rdf_swrl_roundtrip`); kernel-verified data/individual roll-up, smart partial grounding, lazy CEGAR grounding, multi-rule concrete fixpoint chaining, expanded `swrlb:*` built-ins with unconditional `builtinToDataRange_real_iff`, SWRL strict declaration checks, inferred `DataPropertyAssertion` materialization, and SWRL-aware justifications; generalized $n$-ary and nominal-anchored GCI absorption, dependency-directed backjumping, input-scaled SROIQ(D) world limits, and parallel non-EL classification, realization, and Reiter HST justifications with kernel-proved worker-count invariance.
- **G. Fast ABox Classification, Realization, and Materialization (`v1.2.1`–`34f4c67`)** (`FastClassification/{EL,Reference,Runtime}.lean`, `Materialize.lean`, `SROIQD/Search.lean`, `c125089`, `7d492e1`, `34f4c67`): standardized `sabrina-result-v1` CLI output and split fast classification modules; TBox disjoint-component fast path (`TBoxFastCtx`, `counterWithoutIndividuals`, nominal-right GCI simplification, component batch checking) for batch seeding, sat/subsumption queries, and `SubObjectPropertyOf` on large ABoxes; strictly monotone positive ABox forward-chaining closure (`computePositiveABoxClosure`), 2-hop ego-neighborhood monotonicity locality (`ABoxEgoIndex`), kernel-checked soft-negative countermodel refutation (`proposeSoftIndividualModel`), and two-phase role witness harvesting.
- **H. Consequence-Based (Horn-$\mathcal{SHIQ}$) Saturation Engine & Zero-Tableau Complete Exit** (`FastCB/{Normalize,Saturate,Canonical}.lean`, `FastCB.lean`, `FastClassification/Runtime.lean`, `tests/SabrinaTests/FastCBTests.lean`, `benchmarks/fast-locality/bench_fast_cb_scale.py`, `a993361`–`v1.2.3`):
  - **H.1 (`FastCB/Normalize.lean`):** integer-interned Horn-$\mathcal{SHIQ}$ extractor and normalizer with converse-paired role IDs, binary role-chain decomposition, transitive-universal expansion (`expandTransAll`), `isPureHornSHIQ` detection, and kernel-proved `extractHornSHIQ_sound`.
  - **H.2 (`FastCB/Saturate.lean`):** worklist-driven Horn-$\mathcal{SHI}$ context saturation (`init`, conjunction, existential, universal, inverse universal, role hierarchy/chains/reflexivity, bottom propagation) with parallel linear-time certificate verification (`checkCBSound`, `checkCBSound_holds`, `runFastCB_subs_sound`, `runFastCB_unsats_sound`, `runFastCB_workers_eq`).
  - **H.3 (`FastClassification/Runtime.lean`):** KP enhanced-traversal seeding from verified CB subsumption and unsatisfiability lower bounds before batch model seeding (`fpeo.ttl` 8w classification down to ~2.3–2.4 s).
  - **H.4 (`FastCB/Saturate.lean`, `FastCB/Canonical.lean`, `FastClassification/Runtime.lean`):** Horn-$\mathcal{SHIQ}$ `maxOne` sibling-merge (`LinkJust.siblingMerge`) and inverse parent-child merge (`SubJust.parentMerge`) rules for `FunctionalObjectProperty`, `InverseFunctionalObjectProperty`, and qualified `≤1 R.B`/`=1 R.B`; parallel kernel-verified canonical model checker (`CBCanonicalModel`, `checkCBCanonicalModel_sound`, `checkCBCanonicalModel_counter_sub`, `checkCBCanonicalModel_witness_sat`) and zero-tableau complete classifier `classifyFastCB` (`classifyFastCB_workers_eq`) wired ahead of KP traversal.
  - **H.5 (`FastCB/Canonical.lean`, `FastCB/Saturate.lean`, `benchmarks/fast-locality/bench_fast_cb_scale.py`):** $O(\text{support})$ inverted-index model checking (`clsNodes`, `roleOutNodes`, `roleInNodes`, `binSearchContains`, `candidateNodes?`, `roleSourceNodes?`), active-node-indexed RBox closure, dynamic context/link budgets for 10k–50k+ class TBoxes (both pure Horn-$\mathcal{SHIQ}$ and non-Horn lower-bound mode), 1k–50k class Horn-$\mathcal{SHIQ}$ scale benchmark (5k in 0.23 s, 10k in 0.46 s, 25k in 3.57 s, 50k in 9.13 s / 255 MB RSS with zero tableau calls), and real-world corpus validation (`bfo-core` 0.02 s, `cob` 0.02 s, `ro` 1.57 s with 25 SWRL rules, `cl-basic` 1.20 s, `go-basic` 1.28 s, `uberon-basic` 7.13 s, FPSO-BS TBox 2.39 s / ABox 3.76 s).
  - **H.6 (`v1.2.3`):** Verified bottom-locality and disjoint-union partial models for nearly-Horn and mixed TBox/ABox classification. Added checked repair for disjunctions and qualified cardinalities, and deferred role-chain expansion until existential expansion had run. Release benchmarks are recorded in the changelog.

## Planned milestones
- **I. ABox Individual Abstraction (Summary ABox):** collapse structurally equivalent named individuals by type/role-neighborhood signature into representative summary nodes for ABox consistency and realization, scaling from ~1k to 50k–500k individuals while refining only clashing summaries via kernel-verified counterexample expansion.
- **J. Integer-Interned Tableau Core & Bitset Labels (`SROIQD/Search.lean`, `SROIQD/Model.lean`):** replace string-IRI lookups and list scans inside canonical-model search, model verification, and RDF serialization with integer-interned IDs and packed array/bitset representations for a 3–5× constant-factor speedup across all expressive workloads.
  - Reduce the cost of exact classification on large nearly-Horn ontologies whose remaining subsumption candidates require tableau search, including `uberon-base.owl`.
