# Roadmap

This document tracks completed and planned milestones for SABRINA. Every Lean module builds with zero `sorry` and strictly standard Lean axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`), checked by `scripts/verify_axioms.py`.

## Completed milestones

- **A. Strict W3C §11.2 validator** (`StrictOWL2DL.lean`, `8e8a679`): `--strict-owl2dl` and `"strict_owl2dl": true` enforcing `owl:topDataProperty` placement, anonymous-individual forest and single-named-root restrictions, XSD 1.1 pattern syntax, and regular/acyclic role hierarchies.
- **B. Declarations, punning, FSS, and import catalogs** (`FunctionalSyntax.lean`, `StrictTyping.lean`, `ImportCatalog.lean`, `426516b`): `Declaration(...)` and annotation-axiom parsing, prefixed ontology/version IRIs, Unicode and `PN_LOCAL_ESC` tokens, W3C §5.8.1–§5.8.2 typing and punning checks, and OASIS `catalog-v001.xml` / `--import-dir` loading.
- **C.1. Unicode 17.0.0 XSD 1.1 regular expressions** (`UnicodeCategories.lean`, `8e8a679`): `\d`/`\D`, `\w`/`\W`, `\p{...}`/`\P{...}` categories and blocks, nested character-class subtraction, and full-Unicode boundary sampling.
- **C.2. Standards correspondence** (`StandardsCorrespondence.lean`, `XMLCorrespondence.lean`, `RegexGrammarCorrespondence.lean`, `65b241f`): independent declarative specifications and correspondence theorems for `xsd:hexBinary`, IEEE-754 ties-to-even rounding, `xsd:dateTime`, RFC 4647 language ranges, Exclusive XML Canonicalization, and the XSD 1.1 Appendix G regex grammar. Renamed primary CLI to `sabrina-query`.
- **D. RDF/XML, Turtle, reasoning services, and W3C suite** (`RDF/`, `ExtendedQueryDecision.lean`, `Classification.lean`, `benchmarks/w3c-owl2-conformance/`, `a0ca51b`): native RDF 1.1 graph, RDF/XML 1.1 and Turtle 1.1 parsers, W3C OWL 2 RDF-to-Structural mapping with round-trip proof, 4-way entailment across 29 axiom forms and anonymous-individual queries, certified TBox classification and ABox realization, and a 42-test conformance suite.
- **E.1. Hash-indexed $\bot$-locality extraction** (`FastLocality.lean`, `benchmarks/fast-locality/`, `e8bd449`): inverted signature index, worklist fixpoint, and linear certificate verifier proved axiom-set-identical to `Profile.extractBotModule` (`extractBotModuleFast_mem_iff`, `extractBotModuleFast_entails_iff`), wired into `sabrina-query` and cached in `sabrina-session`.
- **E.2. Release hygiene** (`v1.0.0`): package versions aligned to `1.0.0`, documentation updated, and `v1.0.0` tagged on GitHub.
- **E.3. Materialization, fast classification, and minimal justifications** (`Materialize.lean`, `RDF/Serialize.lean`, `FastClassification.lean`, `Justification.lean`, `v1.1.0`): kernel-verified inferred OWL export (`.ofn`/`.ttl`/`.owl`), certified hybrid TBox classification, and QuickXplain + Hitting-Set Tree minimal justifications.
- **E.4. Parallel $\mathcal{EL}^{++}$ engine, RDF metadata, and exact reasoning scaling** (`FastEL.lean`, `OWLMapping.lean`, `WiderDataReduction.lean`, `DataRangeExact.lean`, `DataCSP.lean`, `SROIQOps.lean`, `SROIQSearch.lean`, `af2d378`, `v1.1.1`, `ca72069`): parallel integer-interned single-model $\mathcal{EL}^{++}$ classification (350k classes), W3C Table 4/5 ontology-header annotations and two-phase import-closure declaration sharing, kernel-verified pruned Venn partitions and empty-interval filtering, indexed role closure and tableau initialization, and streamed materialization.
- **F. Kernel-Verified DL-Safe SWRL Rules** (`SWRL.lean`, `SWRLBuiltins.lean`, `SWRLReduction.lean`, `FunctionalSyntax.lean`, `RDF/OWLMapping.lean`, `RDF/Serialize.lean`, `Materialize.lean`, `CLI/OFNQuery.lean`, `CLI/SessionMain.lean`, `tests/SabrinaTests/SWRLTests.lean`): orthogonal DL-Safe SWRL AST, Direct Semantics, and DL-safety validator (leaving `Sabrina.OWL2.Axiom` untouched); W3C FSS (`DLSafeRule`), Turtle, and RDF/XML (`swrl:Imp`) parsing and serialization with kernel-checked RDF round-trip (`owl2_rdf_swrl_roundtrip`); kernel-verified local data-variable & facet built-in roll-up (`rollupOneDataVar_holds_iff`), body-only individual-variable tree roll-up (`rollupOneIndVar_holds_iff`), direct single-individual grounding (`encodeSingleIndGroundRule_holds_iff`), nominal-guarded unary encoding (`encodeUnaryRule_holds_iff`), smart partial grounding (`encodeDLSafeRule_holds_iff`, `groundAllRules_sat_iff`, `groundAllRules_entails_iff`, `decideRealWithSWRL_iff`), safe concrete-value functional `swrlb:*` built-ins, verified lazy CEGAR grounding (`decideWithSWRLFast?`), and full CLI/session/materialization/SWRL-aware justification integration.
